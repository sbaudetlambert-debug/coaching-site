01 — Leader

Create clarity.

When intelligence and answers become more accessible, the leader's value no longer rests on expertise alone. What I observe is a shift: less and less the one who knows, more and more the one who clarifies, orients, and enables others to contribute fully.

This is a profound shift in posture. And it doesn’t happen on its own.

In my role as Sales Leaders Excellence Coach at Salesforce, I work daily with first-, second- and third-line leaders on pipeline inspection, deal coaching, clarity of judgment and decision-making under uncertainty.

11 leaders currently in coaching — FY26.

“You ask unexpected questions that help me move forward — thank you.”

— A coachee

Areas of focus

  • Step back and clarify thinking
  • Evolve posture and presence
  • Develop influence and positioning
  • Decide under uncertainty
  • Navigate a role transition

Outcome: Clarity

02 — Team

Build collective intelligence.

A team of skilled people is not automatically a high-performing team. Collective performance rarely depends on individual skills — it depends on the quality of conversations, the ability to voice disagreement, and how intelligence circulates in the group.

These conditions are not imposed. They are built.

I facilitate team development and change communication sessions for leadership teams — from systemic constellation to weekly briefing preparation — to align a team around a transformation.

03 — Organization

Build the capacity to evolve.

AI accelerates change. It doesn't create the capacity to change. Organizations that transform durably don't just evolve their plans — they develop their capacity to change at every level, not just at the top.

The bottleneck is almost never the technology. It is leadership, culture, and how change is carried locally.

I work on large-scale transformation topics, including AI skills development (LEAD AI programme), assessing and building learning paths for leaders. I also serve as an external facilitator for BNP Paribas, within the Transfo/Orga Bivwak! division.
